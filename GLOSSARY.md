# Glossary: DRAM Capacitor Dielectric Etch

Terms are defined as they are used in this book. Chapter references point to the main discussion.

---

## A

**Actinometry:** Normalizing an emission signal by an inert-gas line (Ar 750.4 nm) to remove changes in electron density and temperature. (Ch. 8.2.2)

**Al marker:** The brief Al I 394.4/396.2 nm emission as the 0.3 nm Al₂O₃ insertion clears at mid-film; its centre predicts the clearing time and its width measures the local clearing spread. (Ch. 8.3)

**Al₂O₃ insertion:** The 0.3 nm aluminium oxide layer between the two ZrO₂ layers of ZAZ; interrupts grain boundaries, raises breakdown field, and slows the etch briefly. (Ch. 2.1.2, 12.5)

**ALD edge exclusion:** Edge purge and shadow ring in the ALD chamber that keep the dielectric off the bevel and backside. (Ch. 9.4.1)

**ALE (atomic-layer etching):** Etching by alternating self-limiting modification and removal steps. Plasma ALE (D2) and thermal ALE (T) are used in this book. (Ch. 4)

**Antenna ratio:** Exposed conductor area collecting plasma current divided by the dielectric area it connects to; ≈ 1.3 × 10⁻³ for a capped plate island in D1. (Ch. 13.2)

**Area-selective deposition (ASD):** Deposition that grows on one surface and is blocked on another by an inhibitor. (Ch. 14.2)

---

## B

**BₓClᵧ:** Boron–chlorine film deposited by BCl₃ plasmas where ion bombardment is weak; competes with etching on the floor and protects the plate sidewall. (Ch. 3.3.2, 11.2)

**BCl₃:** Boron trichloride; the oxygen getter that makes chlorination of ZrO₂, HfO₂, and Al₂O₃ exothermic. (Ch. 3.2.2)

**Bevel etch:** Plasma removal of films from the outer millimetre of the wafer and the bevel; needs a BCl₃ step for ZAZ. (Ch. 9.4.2)

**BO band:** Boron monoxide emission (α system, ≈ 420–600 nm); tracks oxygen removed by gettering and falls at clearing. (Ch. 8.2)

**Breakthrough (BT):** Short BCl₃/Ar step at ≈ 100 eV that removes Ti, C, F, and Cl from the ZAZ surface before the main etch. (Ch. 2.4.3, 3.2.4)

---

## C

**Carbon getter:** Carbon (from resist) removing oxygen as CO; helps the cold in-situ route, absent in the hard-mask route. (Ch. 3.2.3)

**Chlorinate and rinse:** Converting a film with involatile cations (Sr, La) to water-soluble chlorides in a plasma, then dissolving them. (Ch. 14.1.3)

**Clearing domain:** The region in which the upper and lower ZrO₂ grains are the same, ≈ 20 nm across, that clears as a unit; ≈ 9 × 10¹⁰ per die. (Ch. 10.1.2)

**Clearing-time spread:** Variation of the local clearing time, √((σ_t/t)² + (σ_R/R)²) at wafer level; σ_g at domain level. (Ch. 6.1, 10.2)

**Cold in-situ route (R0):** The high-k step run as the last step of the plate etch at 60 °C and 150 eV under resist (Book #32). (Ch. 1.6.2)

**Contact chain:** Test structure of 10⁶ periphery contacts in series; counts opens. (Ch. 15.3.3)

**Cut edge:** The face of the ZAZ at the plate edge, under the TE TiN, left after the clear. (Ch. 1.4, 11)

---

## D

**D1:** Reference hot continuous clear: BCl₃/Cl₂/Ar at ≈ 70 eV on a 250 °C chuck; 36 s clear + 25 s overetch. (Ch. 1.8)

**D2:** Reference plasma-ALE clear at 100 °C: BCl₃/Cl₂ chlorination, Ar⁺ at 60 eV; 0.10 nm per cycle, 73 cycles. (Ch. 4.2)

**DMAC:** Dimethylaluminum chloride, Al(CH₃)₂Cl; ligand-exchange reagent for thermal ALE; pyrophoric. (Ch. 4.3, 7.6)

**Damage reach:** Distance from the plate edge over which cell leakage or retention is degraded; ≈ 0.1–0.15 µm for D1. (Ch. 13.7.2)

---

## E

**EOT (equivalent oxide thickness):** Thickness of SiO₂ with the same capacitance; 0.50 nm for the reference ZAZ. (Ch. 1.1.1)

**EPC (etch per cycle):** Thickness removed per ALE cycle; 0.10 nm for tetragonal ZrO₂ in D2. (Ch. 4.2)

**Etch–deposition transition:** Ion energy below which a BCl₃ plasma deposits BₓClᵧ on an oxide instead of etching it. (Ch. 3.3.2)

**EWMA:** Exponentially weighted moving average; filter used for run-to-run control. (Ch. 15.5.2)

---

## F

**Fluorine trap:** Conversion of zirconium wall deposits into involatile ZrF₄ by fluorine cleans, which then fluorinate later wafers. (Ch. 9.2.1)

**Feed-forward (FF):** Using an upstream measurement (ZAZ thickness, phase) to set the recipe (zone temperatures, overetch, cycle count). (Ch. 6.3.2, 15.5.1)

---

## G

**Gaussian tail:** Residue probability from the normal spread of domain clearing times; beaten by overetch. (Ch. 10.2)

**GIXRD:** Grazing-incidence X-ray diffraction; measures the monoclinic fraction on monitor wafers. (Ch. 15.4.1)

**Grain residue:** Crystalline ZrO₂ grain centres left as islands 0.5–1.5 nm thick near the end of clearing. (Ch. 10.1)

---

## H

**Halogen exchange:** Replacement of Zr–F by Zr–Cl through reaction with BCl₃ (forming BF₃); used in the BT and in thermal ALE. (Ch. 3.2.4)

**Hybrid route (W):** Dry clear stopped with ≈ 0.8 nm of ion-damaged ZAZ, finished in dilute HF. (Ch. 4.4.2)

**HZO:** Hafnium–zirconium oxide laminate or alloy, a higher-k variant. (Ch. 14.1)

---

## I

**Inhibitor:** Molecule that blocks ALD nucleation on a surface for area-selective deposition. (Ch. 14.2)

**Island budget:** Allowed residual islands per die (≈ 8) or per cm² of periphery (≈ 20) for < 0.01 contact opens per die. (Ch. 10.3.2)

---

## J

**Johnsen–Rahbek (J-R) chuck:** Electrostatic chuck with a moderately conductive dielectric (doped AlN) for strong clamping at high temperature. (Ch. 5.3.1)

---

## K

**k_eff:** Effective permittivity of the ZAZ stack; ≈ 43. (Ch. 1.1.1)

---

## L

**Lateral recess:** Sideways loss of the plate sidewall conductors in the clear; TE TiN 1.2 nm in D1. (Ch. 11.2)

**Ligand exchange:** Thermal-ALE removal step in which a reagent (DMAC, TMA, SiCl₄, BCl₃) converts a surface fluoride into volatile products. (Ch. 4.3.1)

---

## M

**Micromask:** Local contamination (carbon, boron glass, particle) that shields the ZAZ and leaves an island. (Ch. 10.3)

**Monoclinic fraction:** Share of periphery ZrO₂ grains in the monoclinic phase (≈ 30%); slower to etch. (Ch. 2.2.2)

---

## N

**Non-Gaussian residue:** Residue from mechanisms outside the normal clearing spread (C, B glass, F patches, nodules, particles); little helped by overetch. (Ch. 10.3)

**Nodule:** ZrO₂ particle 10–50 nm thick embedded in the film from the ALD; not removable by the clear. (Ch. 10.3.5)

---

## O

**Overetch (OE):** Etching after the mean clearing time, as a fraction of t_EP; 70% in D1. (Ch. 10.2.3)

**Overlap:** Distance the plate extends beyond the last dummy row; 1.5 µm reference, 0.4 µm proposed. (Ch. 11.6)

**Overlap ladder:** Test arrays with plate edges at 0–0.8 µm from the cells; measures damage reach. (Ch. 13.7.2)

**Over-cycling:** ALE analogue of overetch; 30% in D2. (Ch. 4.2.7)

**Oxide cap:** 60 nm PE-TEOS on the plate; hard mask for the clear. (Ch. 2.3.2, 12.4)

---

## P

**PET (post-etch treatment):** Remote H₂O/N₂ plasma at 250 °C that removes chlorine and makes boron soluble, followed by a DI rinse. (Ch. 12.3)

**Periphery:** Logic and interface circuits outside the array; its top SiN carries the ZAZ to be cleared. (Ch. 1.2)

**Plate island:** One continuous plate per bank, 1.1 × 1.2 mm; 32 per die. (Ch. 1.4)

**Plate-edge comb:** Interdigitated plate edges that measure leakage across the cleared SiN. (Ch. 11.5.3)

---

## R

**Residue tail:** The low-probability end of the domain clearing distribution that sets contact yield. (Ch. 1.5.2, 10)

---

## S

**Seal:** ALD SiN that fills the undercut slot after an isotropic clear. (Ch. 11.4.3)

**Selectivity (ZrO₂:SiN):** 0.75 in the cold route, ≈ 3 in D1, ≈ 8 in D2, > 100 in thermal ALE. (Ch. 3.5, 4.5)

**Slot:** Void 5.4 nm tall under the TE TiN left by isotropic undercut of the ZAZ. (Ch. 11.4.2)

**Split route:** Plate conductor etch, strip, and dielectric clear in separate chambers on one mainframe. (Ch. 5.1, 5.6)

**Stringer:** ZAZ left on vertical walls of topography by a directional etch. (Ch. 10.5)

**Synergy:** Fraction of ALE removal due to the combination of half-steps, (EPC − α − β)/EPC; ≈ 90% in D2. (Ch. 4.2.4)

---

## T

**t_EP:** Mean clearing time detected by endpoint; ≈ 36 s in D1. (Ch. 8.1)

**TE TiN (top electrode):** 5 nm TiN on the ZAZ; exposed at the plate sidewall during the clear. (Ch. 2.3.2, 11)

**Tetragonal ZrO₂:** High-k phase (k 35–45) of ZrO₂, templated on TiN. (Ch. 2.2.2)

**Thermal ALE (T):** Fluorination by HF and ligand exchange by DMAC at ≈ 275 °C; isotropic; needs ≈ 190 cycles. (Ch. 4.3, 10.4.2)

**Threshold energy (E_th):** Ion energy below which removal stops; for ZrO₂ ≈ 60 eV at 60 °C, 25 eV at 250 °C. (Ch. 3.4)

**TXRF:** Total-reflection X-ray fluorescence; production method for average Zr residue. (Ch. 15.2)

---

## U

**Undercut:** Lateral removal of the ZAZ under the TE TiN at the plate edge; ≤ 0.3 nm in D1, 7–10 nm in thermal ALE. (Ch. 11.4)

---

## V

**VPD blind spot:** Failure of HF-vapor collection to dissolve crystalline ZrO₂, so VPD-ICPMS undercounts residue. (Ch. 15.2.2)

**Virtual metrology (VM):** Model that predicts a residue-risk score per wafer from FDC features. (Ch. 15.6)

---

## W

**Waferless clean:** Per-wafer chamber clean in BCl₃/Cl₂ with a cover wafer; never fluorine or oxygen in this chamber. (Ch. 9.2.2)

---

## Y

**Y₂O₃ / YOF coatings:** Chamber surface coatings that resist BCl₃ because YCl₃ is involatile. (Ch. 5.4)

---

## Z

**ZAZ:** ZrO₂ 2.6 nm / Al₂O₃ 0.3 nm / ZrO₂ 2.6 nm capacitor dielectric; 5.4 nm on the periphery. (Ch. 2)

**Zr cross-contamination:** Transfer of zirconium to shared tools via backsides, bevels, and chucks; limit 1 × 10¹⁰ /cm² on backsides. (Ch. 9.3)

**ZrCl₄:** Etch product; vapor pressure ≈ 26 Torr at 250 °C, 10⁻⁵ Torr at 60 °C. (Ch. 3.1.3)

**ZrF₄:** Involatile fluoride (subl. ≈ 905 °C); forms a passivating skin in fluorine plasmas. (Ch. 3.1.2)
