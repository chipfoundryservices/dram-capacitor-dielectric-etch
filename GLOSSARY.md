# Glossary: DRAM Capacitor Dielectric Etch

Terms are defined as they are used in this book. Chapter references point to the main discussion.

---

## A

**Al marker:** The transient Al emission as the 0.3 nm Al₂O₃ layer at mid-ZAZ etches; predicts each wafer's clearing time. Weaker at the edge etch, stronger in the hot clear. (Ch. 8.2)

**ALD wrap-around:** Deposition of the ZAZ into the gap between the wafer backside and the pedestal; full thickness to x_sat ≈ 2.1 mm, tailing to about 3.1 mm. (Ch. 2.5)

**ALE (atomic-layer etching):** Etching by alternating self-limiting modification and removal; plasma ALE and thermal ALE. (Ch. 3.4, 6, 13)

**Amorphous ZAZ:** The film as deposited, before the 400 °C top-electrode ALD; etches at 9 nm/min (against 6 for tetragonal) and has no grain boundaries. (Ch. 2.2, 4.5)

**Antenna ratio:** Exposed conductor area divided by the dielectric area it is connected to; 0.0198 for an island with its W top exposed. (Ch. 7.4)

**Aspect ratio (channel):** Depth over diameter of the interstitial channel between three coated pillars; 109 in the reference (13 nm × 1.41 µm). (Ch. 2.5, 6.2)

---

## B

**BCl₃:** Boron trichloride; oxygen getter that makes zirconia chlorination exothermic; converts ZrF₄ but not AlF₃. (Ch. 3.2)

**Bevel:** The rounded outer rim of the wafer, about 0.6 mm of arc on each side; cleared by the edge etch. (Ch. 1.2, 5)

**Boundary (edge etch):** The inner edge of the top-side etched ring at r = 147.0 ± 0.3 mm, set by the upper plate and sharpened by the ion-assisted chemistry. (Ch. 5.4)

**Breakthrough (BT):** Short BCl₃/Ar step at 70 eV that removes the TiOₓNᵧ oxide before the TiN stop. (Ch. 4.3.1)

---

## C

**Canary wafer:** A bare wafer run through a clean-zone tool with a dummy recipe and then measured for Zr. (Ch. 9.5, App. C.8)

**Class V / Class N:** Class V metals have a halide volatile below about 350 °C (Zr, Hf, Al, Ti, Nb, Ta); Class N metals have none (Sr, Ba, La, Y). (Ch. 14.2)

**Clear (periphery clear):** Removal of the ZAZ from the periphery and scribe after the plate is cut (step P4). (Ch. 7)

**Contact chain:** Scribe structure of 2 × 10⁶ contacts per site that detects opens caused by residual ZrO₂; bounds the open rate only after about 45 wafers. (Ch. 15.5)

---

## D

**DMAC:** Dimethylacetamide; the ligand donor of the HF/DMAC thermal ALE of ZrO₂. (Ch. 3.4, 6)

**Dose margin:** The pulse length divided by the saturation time at the bottom of the channel; kept at ≥ 1.5. (Ch. 6.2, 13.4)

---

## E

**Edge etch (Module E):** Resistless edge-plasma removal of amorphous ZAZ from the top edge ring, bevel, and backside edge, before the top electrode. (Ch. 5)

**Edge zone:** Top 3.0 mm, bevel, and backside 3.5 mm: 66 cm² and 9.6 × 10¹⁷ Zr atoms per wafer. (Ch. 2.5.3)

**Endpoint-relative overetch:** Overetch defined as a multiple of the measured clearing time, so that it tracks rate drift. (Ch. 8.2.3)

**EOT:** Equivalent oxide thickness; 0.50 nm for the reference ZAZ, EOT = 3.9 t/k_eff. (Ch. 1.1)

**EPC (etch per cycle):** Removal per ALE cycle; 0.073 nm (amorphous) and 0.047 nm (tetragonal) at 280 °C. (Ch. 3.4)

**Equivalent thickness (ΔT_eq):** The thickness loss that would give the same leakage as a damaged layer: ΔT_eq = w(1 − η). (Ch. 12.4)

**EWMA:** Exponentially weighted moving-average feedback; here on EPC with λ = 0.3. (Ch. 15.7)

---

## F

**Finish (ALE finish):** Thermal ALE of the last ≈ 1 nm of the periphery ZAZ after a dry clear stopped early (route R2). (Ch. 13.5)

**Fluorine memory:** Fluorine released from the walls of a chamber after the W step; the source of ZrF₄ patches on the ZAZ at the stop. (Ch. 10.3)

**Forest:** The free-standing pillar array covered by the ZAZ and later by the plate. (Ch. 2.5)

---

## G

**Grain tail:** The probability that a ZrO₂ grain survives the clear, P = ½erfc(z/√2) with z = overetch / σ_g. (Ch. 2.3, 4.5, 16.3)

---

## H

**Headroom:** The 0.28 nm of equivalent thickness that the dielectric may lose before the weakest cells cross the retention budget. (Ch. 1.1.3, 12.4)

**High-k zone (Zr zone):** Tools whose walls, chucks, or exhaust carry zirconium: ALD, edge, thermal ALE, and hot-clear chambers. (Ch. 9.1)

**Hot clear (P4):** BCl₃/Cl₂/Ar clear at 250 °C and about 80 eV with the plate as its own mask. (Ch. 7.3)

**HZO:** Hafnium-zirconium oxide; ferroelectric or antiferroelectric phases; etch like ZrO₂ but are phase-sensitive. (Ch. 14.6)

---

## I

**Interstitial channel:** The void between three neighbouring ZAZ-coated pillars; 13 nm in diameter in the reference. (Ch. 2.5.1)

---

## K

**k_eff:** Effective dielectric constant of the ZAZ stack; 43. (Ch. 1.1)

---

## L

**Lateral recess (3D DRAM):** Isotropic cutback of the dielectric into each tier's cavity; 15 nm takes 205 cycles. (Ch. 14.8)

---

## M

**Modules E, P, T, C:** The four dielectric etches: edge, periphery clear, trim and rework, ALD-chamber clean. (Ch. 1.3)

---

## O

**Overetch (OE):** Extra etch time after clearing, as a fraction of the clearing time; 35% reference and 40% production for P4. (Ch. 4, 16.3)

**Overlap:** Distance from the last dummy row to the plate edge; 1.5 µm in the reference, minimum about 0.25 µm. (Ch. 11.3)

---

## P

**P1–P5:** The five steps of module P: conductor etch, TiN breakthrough and stop, strip, hot clear, post-etch treatment. (Ch. 7.1)

**Plate as its own mask:** Use of the W/SiGe/TiN island to define the ZAZ clear after the resist has been stripped. (Ch. 7)

---

## Q

**QCM (quartz crystal microbalance):** Witness crystal giving 3.2 Hz per ALE cycle at 6 MHz. (Ch. 8.4.2)

**Queue limit:** Maximum time from the ZAZ deposition to the top-electrode ALD (4 h reference, including modules E and T). (Ch. 1.4.2)

---

## R

**R0–R4:** The five routes for the periphery clear: integrated (Book #32), strip-then-clear, R1 plus ALE finish, all-thermal, hybrid dry plus wet. (Ch. 4.6, 16)

**Rework:** Full strip of the ZAZ by thermal ALE and re-deposition; at most one per wafer; −1.4% C_s each. (Ch. 13.6)

---

## S

**Seal:** What covers the cut dielectric edge after P5: the ILD alone or an ALD liner. (Ch. 11.5)

**Shell (TiOₓ):** Oxide grown on the TiN edge by the strip; 1.8 nm; protects the edge against the hot clear; life 54 s against a 38 s step. (Ch. 4.4.3, 7.2)

**Stop:** The conductor etch P2 that clears 5 nm of TiN and stops on the ZAZ with a loss under 0.3 nm; requires S ≥ 18. (Ch. 4.2, 10)

**Strip (P3):** Downstream O₂/N₂ ash at 250 °C; removes resist and grows the shell and the WOₓ top. (Ch. 7.2)

**Strip-then-clear:** Route R1: the resist is removed before the dielectric clear. (Ch. 7)

---

## T

**TDDB:** Time-dependent dielectric breakdown; Weibull with area scaling and field acceleration. (Ch. 12.5)

**Thermal ALE:** Plasma-free HF/ligand-exchange etching; isotropic and self-limiting. (Ch. 3.4, 6)

**Trim (Module T):** Thermal-ALE removal of 0.07–0.22 nm from an over-thick lot, to nominal and no further. (Ch. 13)

**Trim-dose array:** Capacitor array with 0–4 trim cycles used to calibrate leakage and C_s against cycles. (Ch. 12.7, App. C.9)

---

## V

**Veil:** Thin wall of non-volatile material formed on a resist sidewall; absent in the strip-then-clear route. (Ch. 7.1)

**VPD-ICPMS:** Vapor-phase decomposition with inductively coupled plasma mass spectrometry; floor 10⁸–10⁹ cm⁻²; the proof of 10¹⁰ Zr cm⁻². (Ch. 15.3)

---

## W

**Witness crystal:** ZrO₂-coated quartz or GaPO₄ crystal etched alongside the wafer to track EPC. (Ch. 8.4.2)

---

## Z

**ZAZ:** ZrO₂ 2.6 nm / Al₂O₃ 0.3 nm / ZrO₂ 2.6 nm; 5.5 nm, EOT 0.50 nm. (Ch. 1.1, 2.1)

**ZrF₄ trap:** Zirconium fluoride sublimes only near 906 °C, so fluorine on a zirconia surface or wall forms a skin that does not leave. (Ch. 3.2.2, 9.4.2, 10.3)
