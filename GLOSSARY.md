# Glossary: DRAM Capacitor Dielectric Etch

Terms are defined as they are used in this book. Chapter references point to the main discussion.

---

## A

**Actinometry:** Normalizing an emission line by an inert-gas line (Ar 750.4 nm) to remove changes in electron density and temperature; used for the Cl/Ar wall-state ratio. (Ch. 8.1, 9.2)

**AFE / FE HZO:** Antiferroelectric or ferroelectric hafnium–zirconium oxide; its useful properties depend on a metastable phase that etch damage and thinning can disturb. (Ch. 14.5)

**Al marker:** The transient Al emission as the etch front crosses the 0.3 nm Al₂O₃ insertion, used to measure each wafer's rate and adapt the main-step time. (Ch. 8.3)

**ALD seal:** The susceptor feature, with an edge purge, that stops the dielectric's wrap onto the backside at r ≈ 147.5 mm. (Ch. 2.6)

**ALE (atomic-layer etching):** Etching by alternating self-limiting modification and removal steps; measured in nm per cycle. (Ch. 4)

**ALE window:** The range of removal-step ion energy (45–75 eV for ZrO₂) over which EPC is constant: high enough to remove the modified layer, low enough not to sputter the film beneath. (Ch. 4.1.3)

**Area scaling (Weibull):** η(A) = η(A₀)(A₀/A)^(1/β); a die's 21 cm² of dielectric has a characteristic life ≈ 150× shorter than a 10⁻³ cm² test array's. (Ch. 13.5)

---

## B

**BCl₃:** Boron trichloride; the oxygen getter that makes chlorination of ZrO₂, HfO₂, Al₂O₃, and TiO₂ exothermic; deposits BₓClᵧ below E₀. (Ch. 3.2–3.3)

**Bevel:** The rounded outer edge of the wafer, from the front shoulder (r ≈ 149.6 mm) over the apex to the back shoulder. (Ch. 2.6)

**Bevel removal (module 1):** Confined-plasma removal of TE TiN and ZAZ from the front ring (r ≥ 148.8 mm), apex, and backside ring (r ≥ 147.0 mm). (Ch. 7)

**Bevel rinse:** Single-wafer dilute-HF dispense on the backside and bevel, with the front protected by N₂; the fallback when bevel Zr is above alert. (Ch. 7.5)

**Boron ring:** BₓClᵧ deposited in the bevel boundary's taper where the ion energy passes through E₀; removed by the pre-clean before the SiGe fill. (Ch. 11.4.2)

**Bottom-to-top ratio:** Trim at the channel bottom divided by trim at the top; ≈ 0.92 in the pilot, limited by soft saturation. (Ch. 12.3.3)

**BₓClᵧ / BOₓClᵧ:** Boron-chlorine and boron-oxychlorine films deposited by BCl₃ plasmas; hygroscopic; hydrolyse to boric acid and HCl. (Ch. 3.2.3, 3.7)

**BₓClᵧ micromasking:** Islands of boron-chlorine deposit that shadow the ZrO₂ beneath them during the main step and leave residue. (Ch. 10.2.4)

---

## C

**Channel (between pillars):** The open passage between three neighbouring pillars after the dielectric; its radius at the top sets how much TE TiN reaches the bottom. (Ch. 1.3.3, 12.1)

**Clamp (wafer-wide):** The limitation of the voltage across the dielectric by its own leakage; 2 m² of ZAZ clamps the bevel-plasma charging near 1.2 V. (Ch. 13.2)

**Clearing curve (ALE):** The per-cycle Zr emission during the finish, proportional to the fraction of the periphery still covered; gives n₅₀ and n₉₈. (Ch. 8.4)

**Clearing-time map:** The local time the continuous step would take to clear the ZAZ, t(r,θ)/R(r,θ); ±5% (3σ), slowest at the edge. (Ch. 6.1)

**Cover-wafer WAC:** A waferless auto-clean run with a dummy wafer on the chuck, so the chuck does not collect zirconium to pass to product backsides. (Ch. 9.4.4)

**Cycle ladder:** Monitor wafers run with deliberately reduced ALE cycle counts to measure the residue tail's σ and R_slow from contact chains. (Ch. 15.2.4)

---

## D

**DMAC:** Dimethylaluminium chloride, AlCl(CH₃)₂; the ligand-exchange reagent of the thermal ALE trim; its chlorine makes ZrO₂ products volatile. (Ch. 4.3)

---

## E

**E₀ (net-zero energy):** The ion energy at which BCl₃ deposition balances ion-assisted removal; ≈ 60 eV for tetragonal ZrO₂ at 60 °C. (Ch. 3.3.2)

**Edge-intensive arrays:** Capacitor arrays with plate edges deliberately close (0.2–1.5 µm) to active cells, to measure edge damage. (Ch. 11.6)

**EOT (equivalent oxide thickness):** 3.9 × t / k; 0.50 nm for the reference ZAZ, 0.475 nm for the trimmed pilot. (Ch. 1.1, 2.1.3)

**EPC (etch per cycle):** Thickness removed per ALE cycle; 0.10 nm (plasma ALE) and 0.06 nm (thermal ALE) for tetragonal ZrO₂. (Ch. 4)

---

## F

**First-cycle effect:** The extra removal (≈ 0.05 nm) in the first ALE cycle on a surface already chlorinated and borated by the main step. (Ch. 4.6)

**Fluorination (thermal ALE):** Conversion of the ZrO₂ surface to a ZrF₄ layer by HF; partly self-limiting (soft saturation). (Ch. 4.3)

---

## G

**Grain-boundary groove:** Excess thinning along grain boundaries during the trim; self-limits at ≈ 0.25 nm because DMAC cannot reach the bottom of a narrower slot. (Ch. 12.4)

**Grain site:** A grain-sized (≈ 20 nm) area under a periphery contact; ≈ 1.2 × 10⁸ per die. (Ch. 10.2.1)

---

## I

**IEDF:** Ion-energy distribution function; must fit within the ALE window. Sinusoidal 13.56 MHz bias puts 59% of 60 eV Ar⁺ outside it; a tailored waveform less than 2%. (Ch. 5.3)

**Ignition transient:** The tens of milliseconds after each ALE plasma ignition, during which the match settles and plate islands may charge; the bias is delayed until it passes. (Ch. 5.5, 13.3.2)

---

## L

**Ligand exchange:** The thermal-ALE removal step in which DMAC transfers Cl and CH₃ ligands to surface Zr and takes F, forming volatile products. (Ch. 4.3.1)

---

## M

**Main step:** The timed continuous BCl₃/Cl₂/Ar step of module 2 (45 s, 150 eV, 4.44 nm), designed never to clear the film. (Ch. 3, 10)

**Module 1 / 2 / 3:** Bevel and backside removal / periphery clear / in-array trim (pilot). (Ch. 1.4)

---

## N

**n₅₀, n₉₈:** The ALE cycles at which 50% and 98% of the periphery have cleared, from the clearing curve; ≈ 11 and ≈ 18 in the reference. (Ch. 8.4)

**Notch (TE TiN):** Lateral recess of the top-electrode TiN under the SiGe at the plate foot; ≈ 1.3 nm in the reference, ≈ 4 nm in the continuous route. (Ch. 11.1)

---

## P

**Periphery clear (module 2):** Removal of the TE TiN and ZAZ from the periphery and scribe lines at the end of the plate etch: TiN clear, main step, ALE finish, strip and treatment. (Ch. 10)

**PEZ (plasma-exclusion zone):** Insulating plates or rings 0.35–0.4 mm from the wafer that keep the bevel plasma off the front and back surfaces. (Ch. 7.2)

**Plate edge:** The edge of each plate island; the ZAZ there lies on SiN with no bottom electrode beneath, so its damaged face is unbiased. (Ch. 11.1–11.2)

---

## Q

**Quasi-ALE:** Alternating partially self-limiting steps with larger EPC (0.2–0.4 nm) and lower synergy; a throughput compromise. (Ch. 4.5)

---

## R

**R_slow:** The film remaining after the main step at the slowest site of the clearing-time map; 1.34 nm in the reference; sets the cycle count. (Ch. 6.2)

**Residue tail:** The distribution of the last grains to clear; specified at P ≤ 8 × 10⁻¹¹ per grain site at the slowest site. (Ch. 10.2)

**Ring-hour compensation:** Raising the ALE cycle count with edge-ring RF-hours, N(h) = 36 + ⌈max(0, h − 50)/100⌉. (Ch. 6.4, 15.5.4)

---

## S

**σ_r:** The persistent grain-to-grain relative rate spread of the continuous step; 7.5% at 150 eV and 60 °C; the main lever on the cycle count. (Ch. 3.4, 10.3.2)

**Soft saturation:** Fluorination that continues slowly with exposure, EPC = EPC₀[1 + β ln X]; makes the trim top-heavy. (Ch. 12.3.3)

**Staircase (bevel boundary):** The stepped termination of TE TiN (≈ 148.6 mm) inside the ZAZ (≈ 148.8 mm) after module 1. (Ch. 11.4.1)

**Synergy:** S = (EPC − α − β)/EPC; 96% for the reference plasma ALE. (Ch. 4.1.2)

---

## T

**Tailored waveform:** A bias voltage waveform that holds the sheath voltage nearly constant, narrowing the IEDF to ≈ 10 eV. (Ch. 5.3.4)

**TE (top electrode):** The 5 nm ALD TiN over the dielectric; continuous over the whole wafer at module 1. (Ch. 2.3.2)

**TiN clear:** The 10 s Cl₂/Ar step that removes the TE TiN from the periphery and starts the main-step clock. (Ch. 3.6, 8.2)

**Trim (module 3):** Thermal-ALE removal of 1.0 nm from the top ZrO₂ of a 6.5 nm ZAZ inside the array (1c pilot). (Ch. 12)

---

## V

**Veil:** A zirconium-rich deposit on the resist sidewall at the plate edge, left free-standing after the strip if ≳ 1 nm thick. (Ch. 10.6)

**VPD-ICP-MS:** Vapour-phase decomposition with droplet scanning and mass spectrometry; measures Zr on the bevel and backside to ≈ 10⁸ atoms/cm². (Ch. 15.3.3; App. F.3)

---

## W

**WAC (waferless auto-clean):** The clean after every wafer: Cl₂/BCl₃ (Zr, Al, Ti, B), then O₂ (carbon), then a BCl₃/Cl₂ season; never fluorine on zirconium. (Ch. 9.3)

---

## Z

**ZAZ:** ZrO₂/Al₂O₃/ZrO₂ capacitor dielectric, 2.6/0.3/2.6 nm, EOT 0.50 nm, tetragonal after the SiGe fill. (Ch. 2.1)

**ZrCl₄:** The volatile product of ZrO₂ chlorination; ≈ 1 Torr at 190 °C; 1.4 × 10⁻⁵ Torr at 60 °C. (Ch. 3.1, 9.1)

**ZrF₄:** The non-volatile product of ZrO₂ fluorination; why fluorocarbon contact etches stop on ZrO₂ residue and why fluorine must never meet zirconium on a wall. (Ch. 1.3, 3.1, 9.3)
